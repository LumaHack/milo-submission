# Milo v2 (branch `Antoine`)

Antoine's version, built on top of Ali's version on `main`. It lives in new files only: Ali's pages (`/`, `/session`, `/demo`, `/sheet`), his routes and his docs are unchanged, so both versions run side by side. Open v2 at `/v2`.

Everything in `docs/SPEC.md`, `docs/DESIGN.md` and `CLAUDE.md` still applies unless this page says otherwise.

A student can also upload their own course and explain it part by part. That feature has its own page: [COURSES.md](COURSES.md).

## Why a v2

Antoine tested Ali's live session on Sat 14 Nov at 21:35. What he saw, confirmed by the ElevenLabs transcript of that run:

- A turn took about 13 s to redraw (Claude about 6 s, fal about 7 s). The final sheet took about 26 s.
- Milo's question lagged one turn behind. The student said "I think the mitochondria", and Milo then asked which part makes the energy. Approach A speaks the hint from the previous turn.
- Every line opened with "Mmhh… but", and tags such as "[puzzled]" were printed on screen.
- Milo said "Are you still there?" while the student was thinking or watching the board. Those silences were also sent to Claude as turns.
- When the student was stuck, Milo asked the same question again.
- The screen felt busy and did not show which ideas were still missing.

## What changes

| Area | v2 |
|---|---|
| Voice loop | Approach B. The agent calls the browser tool `update_board` when the student finishes a point. The tool has Claude score the turn and write Milo's line, and returns the line. Milo says it word for word. The drawing finishes in the background |
| Speed | The Claude call is tuned for speed (see Timing). fal's fast model draws every live board. The careful model draws only the polished sheet at the end |
| Modes | Picked on the start screen. **Coach**: Milo climbs a hint ladder when the student is stuck. **Exam**: Milo only asks open questions and never hints |
| Hint ladder | 0 a vague question. 1 point at the board, which now shows an empty dotted outline with a "?" where the missing part goes. 2 an everyday comparison. 3 two words to choose from. 4 Milo says the word, never what it does |
| Patience | Turn eagerness patient, the longest turn timeout, the skip-turn tool, never "are you still there". Silent or "..." turns are never scored |
| Structure | A checklist of the key ideas, written as questions, each with a status: not yet, getting there, got it, check this. Exam mode shows only how many are left |
| Pause | Pause hangs up the voice and keeps the board. Resume reconnects and Milo says welcome back. Reopening the page offers: pick up where you left off, or start fresh |
| End page | "Aha!": the final sheet, tidied by the careful model once every idea is clear, what you nailed in your own words, what to work on as questions, and every board in order to click through or replay |
| History | "How World War II started", drawn as a chain of falling dominoes: no people, flags, symbols or war scenes. Goes to `main` only if the team agrees. A second topic, "How World War I started", tells it through the alliances on the same dominoes (added on 15 Nov, see "Timing") |

## Run it

1. `.env.local` needs `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`, `FAL_KEY` and `ELEVENLABS_API_KEY`. Milo v2 does not need an agent id there.
2. `pnpm dev`, then create or update Milo v2's agent once: `curl -X POST http://localhost:3000/api/v2/elevenlabs/setup`. Run it again after changing `src/lib/v2/agent.ts`.
3. Open http://localhost:3000/v2 in Chrome, pick a subject, a mode and a topic, allow the microphone. Wear headphones.

To test without a microphone, add one of these to the session address and type turns in the browser console:

- `&voice=text`: a typed chat with Milo's real agent, over a websocket. `window.miloSay("The Treaty of Versailles punished Germany.")` sends a turn; Milo calls `update_board`, the board redraws, his reply shows in the bubble. This tests the whole loop except sound. The skip_turn check-in does not run in a typed chat.
- `&voice=off`: no Milo at all. `window.miloSay(...)` scores a turn directly. `window.miloHear(...)` acts as if the voice agent heard a turn and Milo then did nothing, to test the safety net.

## Milo v2's agent

Created by `/api/v2/elevenlabs/setup` from `src/lib/v2/agent.ts`, named "Milo v2", separate from Ali's agent so both versions keep working. The token route finds it by name.

- LLM `claude-sonnet-5-5`, which ElevenLabs recommends for agents that call tools.
- Client tool `update_board`: waits for the browser's answer (30 s timeout), always says a few words before it runs ("Ooh, wait, I'm drawing that").
- `skip_turn` system tool, only when the student clearly asks for time ("wait", "let me think"). A pause, an "um" or a sentence that trails off ends the turn. After 15 s of silence Milo checks in calmly, never with "are you still there". Turn eagerness patient, turn timeout 30 s.
- Safety net in the page: if Milo has not called `update_board` 6 s after the student's turn, the page scores it and redraws the board itself, then passes Milo his line as a note.
- **Fixed Sun 15 Nov, found by the spec check with the real engine and a stubbed network.** If Milo's `update_board` call arrived after the safety net had already scored the turn, the same words were added as a second turn and scored again: two Claude calls, and by the brain's own rule a repeat that improves nothing climbs the hint ladder. Now a tool call whose words match the turn the safety net just scored is recognised, nothing is scored, and Milo gets the line that was waiting for him. **Not tested by voice.**
- A tap during "Finishing the drawing…" no longer wakes Milo. Before, any second click started a new conversation while the session was ending.
- A turn that only asks for time ("Wait, let me think", "hold on a second") is treated like a silence: it is never scored and never starts the safety net. Before Sun 15 Nov the safety net scored it, which cost the student a step on the hint ladder for asking to think. A turn that goes on to explain something ("Wait, the nucleus holds the DNA") is scored as usual. Checked with typed turns. **Not tested by voice.**

**Fixed Sat 14 Nov, 23:00.** In Antoine's first voice test, he gave a long answer that trailed off ("So, that's, I think, the-"). Milo called `skip_turn`, because the prompt said to stay quiet when a student stops mid-sentence, and waited for him to speak again while he waited for Milo. The page only scored a turn once Milo spoke, so nothing was drawn either. Both are fixed. Checked in a typed chat with his exact words: Milo called `update_board`, said Claude's line word for word, and used `skip_turn` only for "Wait, let me think".
- First message `{{opening_line}}` and `{{topic_about}}`, set per session by the browser.
- Speech-to-text keywords for every topic's hard names, from "mitochondria" to "Sudetenland".
- Expressive tags such as "[curious]" are stripped before they reach the screen.

### A second agent to compare: "Milo v2 words"

Milo v2's agent listens for one fixed list of hard words. A course brings its own ("thylakoid", "Anschluss"), and they are not on that list. ElevenLabs lets a conversation bring its own speech-to-text keywords, if the agent allows it. Changing the tested agent blind was too risky the night before the demo, so there is a second one.

- **What it is**: the same prompt, tool, voice and settings as "Milo v2", plus one switch: a conversation may set `asr.keywords`. Created on Sun 15 Nov with `curl -X POST "http://localhost:3000/api/v2/elevenlabs/setup?variant=words"`. That call only reads the shared tool's id and never touches "Milo v2".
- **How to use it**: add `&agent=words` to a session address. The page then asks for a token for "Milo v2 words" and starts the conversation with the topic's own keywords, at most 24.
- **Checked**: the agent exists with the switch on and the same tool, model and turn settings, and "Milo v2" was not modified (read back from ElevenLabs).
- **Not tested**: no conversation has been started with it, typed or spoken, because the free plan had under five minutes of voice left. If ElevenLabs refuses the keywords, the session shows "Milo's voice hit a problem" and the address without `&agent=words` works as before. Only a person can say whether hard words are heard better.

- **Milo's speech bubble** sits beside his picture and changes with what is selected: the subject, the mode, "Your courses", or the topic under the pointer ("Great! The block keeps one height (176 px, 208 px on a phone): the cards below never move when Milo's line changes. Measured: 0 px on hover and on focus, at 1440 px and 375 px.

- **Topic cards**: a topic with a saved session in the chosen mode shows "2 of 5 explained. Pick up where you left off". Once every idea is explained it shows "All 5 explained. See your sheet" and opens the end page instead of the session.

## The new session screen

`src/components/v2/session-view-v2.tsx`, layout from Antoine's mockups, look from the v3 tokens. A "Classic look" button in the header switches to Ali's session screen running the same session, and a "New look" button switches back. The choice is remembered.

**Classic look does not lead to the end page.** It is Ali's screen, unchanged: its "End session" goes to `/` and its "Open revision sheet" to `/sheet`, which shows Ali's own saved session, not this one. For a demo, make sure the browser is on New look.

- **Header**: the four crayon levels as steps, Classic look, End session.
- **What Milo gets** (left): one row per key idea, as its gap question, with Not yet, Getting there, Got it or Check this. In coach mode the gap Milo is asking about is highlighted. **Hide what's left** blurs the ideas not started yet (the text under the blur is scrambled). On by default in exam mode.
- **Board** (middle, biggest) with "Milo is drawing…" while fal works.
- **Milo** (right, overlapping the board card's edge, never the drawing): his line, one quiet line saying what he is doing, and Milo himself as a 3D cut-out standing beside the board, cut at the waist by the voice bar like a desk in front of him. He changes pose with what he is doing: listening, thinking (while Claude scores and fal draws), talking, pointing at the board (when his line gives a hint), and cheering (for 2.6 s when he grows up a level, and when everything is clear). Poses cross-fade; he still breathes, leans and bobs with his voice.
- **Voice bar**: mute, what you said last, Pause or Resume, Start again (asks to confirm), session clock. Pause hangs up the voice and stops the clock; Resume reconnects and Milo says welcome back.
- **Timeline**: every board with its time and a name ("First explanation", "One more idea clear", "Hint 1", "Final board"), click to look back, Replay.
- **Pick up where you left off**: the session is saved in the browser after every turn. Opening the same topic and mode again offers to pick up or start fresh.
- **One tab at a time**: if the same session is opened in a second tab, the older tab pauses, saves and says "Paused: this session is open in another tab." Pressing Resume there pauses the other one in turn. Two tabs can never save over each other. Checked with two tabs and typed turns.

- **End session** (header) and **See your sheet** (voice bar, appears once every idea is clear) both open the end page. If the last drawing is still on its way from fal, the button reads "Finishing the drawing…" and waits for it, at most 30 s, so the end page always has the final board.

## The end page

`/v2/end?topic=<id>&mode=<coach|exam>`, in `src/components/v2/end-view.tsx`. It reads the session saved in the browser. With no saved session it says so and links back.

- **Header**: the wordmark and the subject line. "Pick another topic", and "Explain it again", which clears the saved session and starts a new one on the same topic and mode.
- **Main card**: "Aha!" when every idea is clear, otherwise "Nice work" with "3 of 5 explained". "Save sheet". Then the final board, big.
- **Milo** at his final age: cheering with "That's all of it! I get it now.", or listening with "I still wonder about 2 things. Come back and tell me?". He never gives an answer.
- **Session summary**: explanations, ideas explained, hints used, time explaining, and the level reached as a chip.
- **What you nailed**: each clear idea as its question, with the student's own words under it.
- **What to work on**: each idea that is not clear, as its question, with Check this, Getting there or Not yet. Never the answer.
- **How your drawing grew**, under the board: every board from left to right with an arrow between them. Click one to see it in the main card ("Back to final board" returns). Replay plays them at 1.4 s each and ends on the final board. The strip scrolls sideways on a phone.
- Under the board, one line says which board is showing: its name, its time and what the student said.
- The two columns keep their own height, so the board does not move when the student's words arrive in the right column. The board is still when the page opens and only lands with its animation when it changes.

**The tidy sheet.** When every idea is clear, the page asks `/api/v2/draw` once, with `final: true`, to redraw the last board with fal's careful model (18.4 s on average over 3 runs). The old board stays up with "Milo is tidying your sheet" until the new picture has loaded. The link is kept with the saved session, so reopening the page costs nothing. If the call fails, the last board stays and nothing is said.

The instruction: "Keep the same drawing, the same layout and the same colours. Make it clean and organised like a finished revision sheet: neat lines, label boxes, and one title top left, spelled exactly: '<the topic's title>'. Give every part the same care: labels the same size, all easy to read. The sheet must carry exactly these labels, in this order, each one written once and in full, spelled exactly as given: '<label>', '<label>', … Erase the old writing and write each label again, straight and horizontal, never sideways, in the place where it was. Nothing else is written on the paper: no repeated or leftover words and no question marks. Do not add parts, shapes, dates or numbers that are not already on the paper. No people, faces, flags, symbols or weapons."

**Why the labels are listed.** The fast model sometimes leaves a garbled or leftover word on a live board. The first tidy instruction said "do not change any word", so the finished sheet kept "Light hits hte (alerib) the leaf". Now every frame keeps the instruction that drew it, the summary call reads those instructions and returns the labels that belong on the sheet after the last one, and the route keeps only labels that an instruction put on the sheet in quotes and that no later instruction removed. Tested on Sun 15 Nov on a history sheet: a label the tidy step had turned into "promised to defand" came back as "promised to defend Poland", and the colours stayed. The careful model still keeps a sideways label sideways, and a stray repeat of two words survived: one run, so look at the sheet before filming it. When the labels cannot be listed (a session saved before this change), the instruction says "Do not add, remove or change any label or word" instead.

**An unfinished board is not tidied.** Tested on Sat 14 Nov with a session at 2 of 5: asked to make it "a finished revision sheet", the careful model added a title of its own, "History revision: 1919-1933", with a year the student never said, and a new question mark. That breaks the rule that the drawing only shows what the student said. So an unfinished session keeps the board as Milo drew it, and the messy board stays honest feedback. To try it the other way, change `complete` in the tidy effect of `end-view.tsx`.

**The summary.** `POST /api/v2/summary`, one Claude call when the page opens (Opus 5.5, low effort, fast mode: 1.4 to 2.0 s over 4 runs, about $0.015). Claude returns, for each clear idea, a turn number and at most 15 words copied from that turn. The route then looks for those words in what the student really said, ignoring case and punctuation, and shows the matching stretch of the student's own turn. A pick that is not found word for word is dropped, and that idea is listed without words. A pick that had to be cut to 15 words ends with "…". So the summary cannot state a fact the student did not say. For a finished session the same call also returns the labels for the tidy sheet (see above), so the tidy step starts when the summary is back. The result is kept with the saved session.

**Your revision sheets.** "Save sheet" is offered once every idea is explained. It adds the sheet to `milo.v2.sheets` in the browser: the picture's link, the title, the nailed ideas with the student's words, and the level after each turn. The button waits for the summary, so a sheet is never saved without the student's words. `/v2/sheets` lists the sheets and opens one in the v3 revision sheet layout: the sheet, "In your words", "How you got here" and Milo. Only the link to the picture is stored. If fal no longer has the picture, the paper says so instead of showing a broken image.

**Why some links load the whole page.** Next 16 keeps a page it navigated away from alive in the background and brings it back for the same address. "Explain it again" then reopened the finished session instead of a new one. So leaving a session through "End session", and every link that starts a session from the end page or a saved sheet, loads the page from scratch.

## Board layouts

A cell, a process, the causes of a war and an event each need a different drawing. Every topic names one of five layouts (`src/lib/v2/layouts.ts`). A built-in topic has its own tested drawing instructions, and its brain prompt is word for word what it was (checked: the cell and World War II prompts are byte-identical before and after this change). A part of a student's course has none, so the brain prompt gives Claude that layout's paragraph, what a hint looks like on it, and its four tested examples, with the rule to copy their layout and never their content.

| Layout | Suits | How the paper is organised | What a hint looks like | Tested with |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| structure | a thing and its parts | one drawing in the middle, labels with arrows | a dotted outline with a question mark | How a cell works (Ali's four prompts) |
| process | steps that follow one another | a chain of boxes joined by arrows, in a row or a loop | an empty box in the chain | Photosynthesis |
| causes | why something happened | a row of dominoes, the outcome after the last one | a blank domino with a question mark | How World War II started |
| event | one event in a place and a time | a timeline of small boxes along the top, a simple map with labelled zones, an outcome box | an empty box on the timeline, or an unlabelled zone | The Great Fire of London |
| comparison | two things side by side | a table with two columns, one row per point | an empty row | Mitosis and meiosis |

**Tested with fal on Sun 15 Nov**, fast model, each layout's four examples drawn in order, each board editing the one before (`GET /api/v2/dev/layout-test?kind=process`, development only). Every board was looked at.

| Layout | Seconds per board | Result |
|---|---|---|
| process | 5.3, 7.2, 7.5, 5.8 | Passed first time. Same sheet, every label right |
| comparison | 6.3, 9.6, 10.3, 7.8 | Passed on the second wording. The first wording let level 3 overwrite the first row, and put the title inside the left column |
| event | 4.1, 6.1, 7.0, 9.7 | Passed on the third wording. Tick marks on a timeline came out with the wrong count and labels in the wrong order, so the timeline is now a row of small boxes. A long label on the final board was split in two, so its labels are now three words at most |

On every board: no people, faces, flags, symbols or weapons. The event layout draws its map as a ring of small houses, which is fine for a town and is still a diagram. One run each, like every fal number in this repo.

**A layout driving a real turn.** A made-up part, "The water cycle" with the process layout, sent to `/api/v2/turn`: turn 1 ("Water goes up into the sky and then it rains") came back as two boxes joined by an arrow, labelled with the student's words only, in 2.2 s. Turn 2 ("I don't know how it gets up there") came back at hint step 1, with a new empty box and a question mark in front of the chain, and Milo pointing at it. Nothing from the photosynthesis examples leaked in.

**Not tested:** a battle or any other war topic on the event layout. The layout says "never a scene: no figures, vehicles or weapons", but only a person looking at the boards can clear it for a demo.

## What "close enough" means

Each key idea in `src/lib/v2/topics.ts` has a **core**, what the student must get across, and a **term**, the proper word. Claude judges meaning, not wording, and an idea can build up over several turns. Dates and numbers beyond the core are a bonus, never required, and a wrong bonus detail does not change the status.

- **Coach mode**: everyday words count. "The skin of the cell decides what gets in and out" is clear.
- **Exam mode**: the term is needed too, in any spelling speech-to-text produces. In everyday words only, the idea is "getting there", and Milo asks what that thing is called.

Checked on Sat 14 Nov with 11 near-miss sentences in both modes: everyday wording counted in coach mode and was asked about in exam mode ("What's that skin called?"); misheard terms ("mighty-condria") counted in both; a wrong answer (Pearl Harbor as the start of the war) was marked "check this" in both, with a puzzled question instead of a correction.

## The v2 turn contract

Code: `src/lib/v2/contract.ts`. It adds to the shared contract in `docs/SPEC.md` without changing it.

- **Request** (`POST /api/v2/turn`): `topicId`, optionally `part` (the definition of a part of the student's own course, see `docs/COURSES.md`), `mode` (`coach` or `exam`), `studentTurns`, and the state before this turn: `previousLevel`, `previousIdeas`, `previousBiggestGapId`, `previousHintStep`, `previousDrawPrompt`, plus `recentMiloLines` so Milo varies how he starts.
- **Result**: `ideas`, `level`, `biggestGapId`, `hintStep` (0 to 4, always 0 in exam mode), `miloLine` (what Milo says next), `said`, `changed`, `drawPrompt`.
- **Draw** (`POST /api/v2/draw`): `prompt`, `previousImageUrl`, and `final`. The fast model draws every live board. `final: true` redraws the last board with the careful model, for the end page.
- **Summary** (`POST /api/v2/summary`, `src/lib/v2/summary.ts`): `topicId`, optionally `part`, `studentTurns`, `ideas` with their statuses, and optionally `drawPrompts` (the instruction that drew each board). Returns `nailed`: one entry per clear idea, with `words` (the student's own, or null), and `labels` for the tidy sheet.
- **Frame**: v2 adds `hintStep`, `clear` and `drawPrompt` to the shared frame.
- **Saved session** (`src/lib/v2/storage.ts`): two optional extras for the end page, `polished` (the tidy picture and the board it was made from) and `summary`. Both are dropped when the session carries on.

**What a night of testing cost.** In development every v2 route adds one line to `node_modules/.cache/milo/usage.jsonl` (git ignores it): the route, how long it took, token counts and dollars for Claude, the endpoint for fal. No keys, prompts or student words.

The rules in `docs/SPEC.md` still hold: the level never drops, `changed: false` means no fal call, one redraw in flight with the newest result winning, and the old board stays up until the new one has loaded.

**When a call does not come back.** The browser gives Claude 40 s for a turn and fal 45 s for a drawing, so one stuck call cannot hold up everything behind it. A drawing that fails is tried once more straight away ("Keep talking, Milo is trying again"). If that fails too, the old board stays, the drawing is owed, and it is drawn again with the next turn even if that turn adds nothing. On the end page the summary gets 30 s and the tidy sheet 75 s. Checked with the engine test for the retry. No real fal failure happened in 74 drawings overnight, so the rest has only been read.

**Every field of a request has a size.** A turn is at most 8,000 characters and a session 200 turns, a drawing instruction 4,000 characters. This only matters if the server is reachable by someone else.

**An idea with no proper term** (possible in a part of a course): exam mode accepts everyday words for it, and in coach mode the hint ladder stops at step 2, because steps 3 and 4 hand over a word and there is none.

**A bare yes or no explains nothing** (parts of a course only, added on 15 Nov). On the first voice test of a real course, Milo asked "is that a real person's wage?", the student answered "No, it's not", the idea was counted as explained, and Milo finished the thought himself. Three rules in the brain prompt now say that the student has to say it in their own words, that Milo says back only what the student said, and that a hint is never a yes-or-no question holding the missing fact. The route also enforces it: when the new turn is only agreement ("Yes, exactly, you're right"), every idea, the level and the board stay as they were, and the hint ladder goes up one step at most. Checked with typed turns: a bare yes changes nothing and Milo asks for it "in your words"; the same answer given in the student's own words counts. Not yet checked by voice. The two built-in topics keep their tested prompt word for word, so this rule does not apply to them yet.

## Timing

Measured on Sat 14 Nov, typed turns sent to `/api/v2/turn` on a laptop, one run per setting.

**Claude scoring one turn**, the four demo turns in coach mode:

| Setting | Average | Slowest |
|---|---|---|
| `claude-opus-5-5`, low effort | 6.8 s | 9.7 s |
| `claude-opus-5-5`, low effort, fast mode | **2.4 s** | 3.3 s |
| `claude-sonnet-5-5`, low effort | 3.1 s | 3.3 s |
| `claude-haiku-5-5`, low effort | 7.4 s | 9.4 s |

Chosen: Opus 5.5, low effort, fast mode. Same model as Ali's version, about three times faster, at twice the price per token. A stuck student in coach mode averaged 2.3 s a turn, and exam mode 1.9 s. If fast mode is rate limited, the route answers at standard speed. Turn it off with `V2_FAST_MODE=0`, or change the model with `V2_ANTHROPIC_MODEL`.

**fal, fast model**: first board 4.2 s, edits 6.4 to 6.9 s.

So a typed turn now takes about 6 to 9 s from text to new board, against 11 to 14 s before. The careful model (about 20 s) only runs once, for the end page.

**History boards**, the four example prompts in `src/lib/v2/topics.ts` drawn in order on Sat 14 Nov: 4.8, 7.1, 6.5 and 6.4 s on the fast model. All four came back as dominoes, with no people, flags or symbols. The fast model may have misspelled "Appeasement" on the dense level 4 sheet. The careful model redrew it in 18.9 s with every label right and the dominoes falling.

**"How World War I started"**, added on Sun 15 Nov at Antoine's request. Five ideas, one per domino: the two alliances, the assassination in Sarajevo, Austria-Hungary and Serbia, Russia and Germany, Belgium and Britain. It has no example prompts of its own: it uses the "causes" layout, so its brain prompt is the one a part of a course gets, with the World War II examples as a model of the layout only. One typed run in coach mode, voice off: six turns of 6 to 12 s, a vague first turn drawn as a scribble of dominoes, a wrong turn ("Austria-Hungary blamed Russia") drawn as said and questioned ("the shooter was a Serb nationalist, so why would they blame Russia?"), then 5 of 5 and level 4, and the end page tidied the sheet with every label spelled right and five quotes word for word. Every board was dominoes and labels only: no people, flags, symbols or weapons. **Not tested by voice.** Its hard words (Sarajevo, Franz Ferdinand, Austria-Hungary) are not in the tested voice agent's keyword list, which was left as it is: speech-to-text may mishear them, and "Milo v2 words" (`&agent=words`) is the agent that takes them. On the start screen it took the place of a placeholder card, after World War II, so the demo card stays first.
